Number Reader

Pick a number in secret. The camera watches your face while the screen shows numbers, and at the end the phone guesses your number from body reactions you don’t control.

The room can be dark: the white screen lights your face. Set screen brightness to maximum, lock rotation to portrait, and hold the phone about 30 cm from your face.

How does it know?

The idea comes from a scientific test called the Concealed Information Test. When the number in your head appears while you deny it, your body reacts in spite of you:

  • Your response time: slows by a tiny amount when you deny the number you know. This is the strongest indicator scientifically.
  • Your pulse: slows for a moment after you see something meaningful to you. The camera sees it from colour changes in your face with each beat.
  • Your blinking: is recorded and shown, but not used in the verdict because the direction of its effect isn’t scientifically consistent.

The verdict won’t be right every time. If no number stands out clearly enough, the page says it can’t tell rather than guessing at random.
