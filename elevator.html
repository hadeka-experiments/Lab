Floor Counter

No barometer, no GPS: the motion sensor feels the elevator push as it starts and brake as it stops, and the page works out how many metres you travelled and which floor you’re on.

Enter the elevator, tap “Start”, and hold the phone still in your hand or put it on the floor. Don’t walk with it.

Rides

    Calibrate on the last ride

    If you know how many floors the last ride was, pick it and the page computes your building’s floor height.

    How does it work?
    • An elevator gives an upward “push” when it starts and a “brake” when it stops. The motion sensor feels both, even if you don’t.
    • The page integrates acceleration into speed and speed into distance. Since tiny sensor errors grow over time, it corrects the calculation assuming the elevator is stopped at the start and end of the ride.
    • Browsers read the sensor about 60 times per second. Expected accuracy is about 0.5–1 m per ride, so the floor can be off by one on long rides. Calibrating improves it.
    • Walking or shaking the phone ruins the calculation. Stairs don’t work.
