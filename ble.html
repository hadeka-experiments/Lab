Bluetooth Test

This page tests one thing: can your browser give a continuous stream of signal strength from nearby Bluetooth devices? If yes, sensing can be built on top of it. If not, that route is closed. (Android: use Chrome. iPhone: use the Bluefy browser.)

2. Find devices

Two methods. Try the first; if it doesn’t work, try the second.

    3. Body test

    Tap a device in the list to select it. Then:

    1. Place the phone and the device 2–3 m apart with nothing between them.
    2. Tap “Start test”. For the first 10 seconds stay out of the line between them; for the next 10 seconds stand exactly between them.
