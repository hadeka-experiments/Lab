Your phone is a laboratory.

Sonar, radar, a single‑pixel camera, a star counter, a sleep lab, a navigator that finds you from the sun. Each experiment uses only the sensors already inside an ordinary phone, and runs entirely in your browser.

Before you start. Open these pages from an https link in an up‑to‑date browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Samsung Internet). Allow the permissions each experiment asks for: microphone, camera or motion sensors. On iPhone, turn the silent switch off for experiments that play sound. AI models download once on first use, so Wi‑Fi helps.

Honesty. Every experiment explains what is really measured, what is inferred, and its limits. None of them is a medical or safety device. Ultrasonic experiments can be heard by children and pets.
