Who’s Holding It?

Everyone holds, moves and walks with a phone differently. The page learns each person’s motion fingerprint from the motion sensors, then recognises who’s holding it.

    Enrol someone new

    Enrolment has 3 stages of 20 seconds each. The same person should hold the phone the whole time.

    How does it know, and what are the limits?
    • Every 2.5 seconds the page computes 24 numbers from the sensors: the angle you hold the phone at, your hand’s tiny tremor at different frequencies, your walking rhythm, and how fast you rotate the phone.
    • It compares these numbers with each enrolled person and picks the closest. If the motion is far from everyone enrolled, it says “Unknown person”.
    • The accuracy shown under the people list is optimistic, because testing and enrolment are from the same day and mood. Enrol each person twice on different days for more stable results.
    • When the phone is resting on something there’s no fingerprint, so the page says it’s resting and doesn’t guess.
    • Data is stored on this phone only.
