Time Slice Camera

A normal camera captures the whole picture at one instant. Here every line of the picture is captured at a different moment, so time itself turns into space: moving people stretch and twist, a hand becomes an octopus, cars become streaks.

What exactly is happening, and what to try?
  • Time right to left: The column on the right is now, and the further left you go the older the moment, up to about 3 seconds ago. Move your hand or turn your head slowly and watch them stretch.
  • Top to bottom: The same idea vertically. Tilt your head left and right and watch your neck become a snake.
  • Centre and edges: The centre is now, and the further from the centre, the further back in time. Anything moving makes ripples like waves.
  • Photo finish: The page captures only one line in the middle of the frame, each moment next to the previous one, like photo‑finish cameras at races. Still things become streaks; only what passes the line appears. Put the phone still facing a street or corridor and watch people and cars appear by themselves.
  • Everything is computed on your phone as you shoot; nothing is uploaded.
