Room 360

Stand in place and turn slowly; the page stitches your camera photos onto a bubble around you. Then turn your phone any way… and your room is there.

How does it work? And honestly, what is not real?
  • The phone's gyroscope and compass know exactly where you are looking. Every time you face a new direction, the page takes a photo and stores it with its direction.
  • At the end the photos are pasted onto the inside of a sphere with you at its center. When you turn the phone, the page turns the same way inside the sphere, so the room feels around you.
  • Honestly: there is no real depth measurement here. The bubble is pasted photos, so if you walk forward you do not get closer to the wall. Real depth from phone motion needs cameras and lidar like the big scanning apps; this is the honest page version of the same feeling. For actual walkable depth, try Room Walk 3D.
  • The stitching runs on your phone itself; no photo is sent to any server.
