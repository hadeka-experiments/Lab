Number Reader

Secretly choose a number from 1 to 9. The camera watches your face, eyes and pulse while the screen shows numbers, and at the end the phone guesses your number. You won’t press any button during the test.

The room can be dark, because the white screen lights your face. Set brightness to maximum, lock rotation to portrait, and keep the phone about 30 cm from your face. Resting it on something is best, since any movement spoils the eye and pulse readings.

How does it know?

The test has 3 stages, about two and a half minutes:

  • Eye calibration (12 s): A red dot moves through 9 positions and you follow it with your eyes, so the page learns what your eyes look like at each position.
  • Free viewing (15 s): All numbers appear and you think about yours and look freely. People tend to look at what’s on their mind more than other things without meaning to, and the page tracks where your eyes were at each moment.
  • Numbers one by one (about 2 minutes): Each number appears 4 times. When yours appears, your pulse slows for a moment and your face makes tiny movements. The camera measures both.

The verdict averages only the clear signals. A weak signal, such as an inaccurate calibration, is dropped. If no number stands out enough, the page tells you it can’t decide.
