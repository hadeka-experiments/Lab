Silent Speech

Move your lips to say a word without making a sound, and the front camera reads your lip movement and writes the word. You teach it the words first, then it recognises them from you.

Teach it a word

Tap “Record”, and when “Say it now” appears, mouth the word silently. Repeat each word at least 3 times. Words with distinct lip shapes (like “yes”, “no”, “water”) are recognised better than similar‑looking words.

    How does it know, and what are the limits?
    • A face‑tracking model outputs about 25 numbers 30 times per second describing your mouth’s shape: how open, rounded, stretched, pursed, and so on.
    • Each word becomes a “curve” of those numbers over time. The page compares the new curve with every recorded one using a method called DTW, which tolerates saying the word faster or slower, and picks the closest.
    • If the curve is far from every word, it says “didn’t understand” instead of guessing.
    • It doesn’t read any word in the world, only the words you taught it, from you. Sounds made inside the mouth (like k, g, h) don’t show on the lips.
    • Words are stored on this phone only.
