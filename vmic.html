Camera Microphone

Sound shakes everything around it by amounts too small to see. A phone camera doesn’t capture a frame all at once; it reads it line by line (rolling shutter), so every frame contains thousands of moments. The page uses this to recover sound from a silent video, with no microphone at all.

Start the rear camera and keep the red box on something with clear lines or text, like a printed page or a crisp packet.

How to try it
  1. Calibration (once): Put the phone on a table with the camera on the paper and tap “Calibrate”. The phone plays a tone for 4 seconds while filming, so it shakes itself, and the page learns how fast the camera reads lines from a tone it already knows.
  2. Listen: Play a loud tone or a song with heavy bass from another speaker or laptop, and put the paper on the speaker or pressed against it. Hold the phone on something that doesn’t touch the speaker, and tap “Listen 4 s”.
  3. If the camera picks up a tone, its frequency is shown along with a clear peak in the chart.

Limits: This is the hardest experiment in the set. With a browser camera the page can recover strong tones and rhythm, but intelligible speech is almost impossible. The recovered sound also has a hum from frame gaps, because the camera doesn’t read while moving from one frame to the next.
