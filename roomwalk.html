Room Walk 3D

Capture one spin in place; on-device AI guesses the depth of every photo and builds your room as a real 3D model. Then walk inside it and get closer to things.

How does it work? And honestly, what is not real?
  • The gyroscope knows where the phone is looking, and the page takes 24 photos covering every direction as you turn in place.
  • The Depth Anything V2 model runs on your phone itself (no photo leaves for any server) and guesses how far every point in every photo is from the camera.
  • The page turns the photos and depths into a colored point cloud in real 3D space: you can walk in it, get closer to things, and the perspective changes correctly.
  • Honestly: the depth is an AI guess from a single photo, not a laser measurement. Relative sizes are approximate, walls can look slightly bent, and seams between photos can show. There is no collision: you can pass through a wall. And the capture must be from one spot — if you walk while capturing, the model breaks.
  • The model downloads only the first time; the browser keeps it.
