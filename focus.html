What does it measure, and what are the limits?
  • Blinking: People blink less when they concentrate. This is well known in attention research, but it varies between people, so comparisons are only made between parts of the same session.
  • Looking away: Moments when your face wasn’t turned toward the screen.
  • Head movement: Lots of movement often means restlessness or boredom.
  • Time: How long you spent on each part, and for text, your reading speed in words per minute.

“Focus” here is a relative average of these signals within the session, not an absolute measure. Green means "you focused here more than on the other parts". With fewer than 3 parts, no rating is produced.
